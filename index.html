FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Are trainers available to guide beginners at P9 Fitness? +

Yes, absolutely! As mentioned by our members in 140+ reviews, our trainers are continuously on the gym floor, precisely providing instructions, correcting posture, and ensuring safe execution of all movements for both beginners and experienced lifters.

What are the daily operating hours of the gym? +

We are open every day from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM. This enables students, working corporate professionals, and early risers to work out according to their personal schedule.

Is P9 Fitness women-friendly and safe? +

Proudly yes! P9 Fitness is woman-owned, maintains a zero-tolerance policy against harassment, and provides safe, respectful training zones and private lockers. We have female trainers on staff and an empowering community of female lifters.

Is parking available at Elite Colossal Building? +

Yes! Accessible two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking is available directly at the Elite Colossal Building premises on Ferguson College Road for all active P9 Fitness members.

Where is P9 Fitness located on FC Road? +

We are located in the landmark Elite Colossal Building on Ferguson College Road, Model Colony, Shivajinagar, Pune — directly opposite the Police Ground and right above Yana Sizzler & Shravan Veg Lovers.

Do you provide customized diet and nutrition guidance? +

Yes! Our certified fitness trainers provide tailored macronutrient and calorie targets aligned with your specific body composition goals, whether your focus is lean muscle growth, weight loss, or athletic stamina.

Do you offer special student discounts? +

Yes! We offer special concession packages for college students from Fergusson College (FC), BMCC, COEP, and SPPU. Simply show your valid college student ID card at our reception desk!

What locker and hygiene amenities are provided? +

Every membership includes individual secure lockers, sanitized changing suites, clean showers, and chilled purified drinking water. The workout floors and machinery are sanitized continuously throughout the day.
